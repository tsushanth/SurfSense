SurfSense - Browser Focus Tracker for ADHD, Remote Workers & Students

Where did your day go? SurfSense tracks where your browser time goes and helps you build real focus habits. Categorize sites, set limits, see patterns, take back attention.

Built For:
• Knowledge workers fighting tab-switching habits
• Students with ADHD who lose hours to YouTube and social
• Remote workers who notice their day evaporating without output
• Anyone who wants to know where their attention is actually going

Track Where Your Time Goes
SurfSense monitors your active browser tabs and tells you exactly which sites you visited and how long you spent. Sites are auto-organized into categories like Social Media, Work, News, Education, Entertainment, and more.

Build Focus Habits
• Set daily limits on distracting sites
• Get gentle nudges when you exceed your own limits
• See weekly trends — is your social media creeping up?
• Block lists for deep-work sessions

ADHD-Friendly Design
• Visual reports that make attention patterns obvious
• Categorization without judgment — see facts, not "you're bad"
• Setup takes 30 seconds, no friction to start
• Works across browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)

What We Collect
SurfSense does send some data to our server to build your reports.
- Browser extension: the website domains you visit (the domain only, not full page addresses or page contents) are sent so they can be sorted into categories like Social Media or News.
- Android app: if you turn on Usage Access, the app reads how long each app on your phone is open today and sends the app IDs (package names) so they can be sorted into categories.
- Your daily time totals per category are stored under a random ID and your device's name, so your other devices can show them. SurfSense does not ask for your name or email.

How It Works:
1. Install the browser extension on your computer
2. Link your phone via a one-time code
3. See real-time activity reports
