Take control of your digital life with SurfSense — the intelligent screen time tracker that helps you understand and improve your device usage habits across all your devices.

In today's always-connected world, it's easy to lose track of how much time you spend on your phone, tablet, or other devices. SurfSense gives you the clarity you need with beautiful, real-time usage insights and cross-device tracking that no other app offers.

KEY FEATURES

Multi-Device Tracking
Link all your devices together with simple device codes and get a complete picture of your digital habits. Monitor screen time across your phone, tablet, and more — all in one place.

Smart Usage Trends
View detailed usage trends over 7, 14, or 30 day periods. Understand how your habits change over time and identify patterns that help you build healthier routines.

Category Breakdowns
See exactly where your time goes with intelligent app category breakdowns. Know at a glance how much time you spend on social media, productivity, entertainment, games, and more.

Daily Usage Statistics
Get clear daily stats including total screen time, daily averages, and peak usage days. Track your progress and celebrate when you meet your goals.

Real-Time Visualization
Watch your usage come to life with a stunning waveform-style visualization that shows your activity in real time. A beautiful and informative way to stay aware of your habits.

Auto-Sync
Set your preferred sync interval and let SurfSense automatically keep your data up to date across all linked devices. No manual effort required.

Quick Stats Dashboard
See your most used app category and key metrics at a glance from the main dashboard. Get the information you need without digging through menus.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Install SurfSense on your device and grant usage access permissions
2. Your screen time data is automatically tracked and organized by category
3. Link additional devices by sharing your unique device code
4. View your combined usage trends, daily stats, and category breakdowns
5. Adjust sync intervals and preferences in Settings to suit your needs
