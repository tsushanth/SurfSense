SurfSense - Screen Time and Browser Focus Tracker

Where did your day go? SurfSense shows where your phone and browser time goes, so you can spot patterns and build real focus habits.

Built For:
• Knowledge workers fighting tab-switching habits
• Students with ADHD who lose hours to YouTube and social
• Remote workers who notice their day evaporating without output
• Anyone who wants to know where their attention is actually going

What You Can Do
• See today: how long you used your phone and which apps you spent it in, grouped into categories.
• Compare weeks: a week view shows each day against the same day last week.
• Correct categories: tap an app to change its category when the automatic one is wrong.
• Link your devices: create a code on one device, enter it on another, and see phone and browser time together. Remove a device whenever you like.
• Track your browser: the Chrome extension sorts the websites you visit into categories like Social Media, Work, News, Education and Entertainment.
• Export your data: save the last 30 days from all your devices as a CSV file.

ADHD-Friendly Design
• Visual reports that make attention patterns obvious
• Categorization without judgment: see facts, not "you're bad"
• Setup takes 30 seconds, no friction to start

What We Collect
SurfSense does send some data to our server to build your reports.
- Browser extension: the website domains you visit (the domain only, not full page addresses or page contents) are sent so they can be sorted into categories like Social Media or News.
- Android app: if you turn on Usage Access, the app reads how long each app on your phone is open today and sends the app IDs (package names) so they can be sorted into categories.
- Your daily time totals per category are stored under a random ID and your device's name, so your other devices can show them. SurfSense does not ask for your name or email.

How It Works:
1. Install SurfSense on your phone and allow Usage Access
2. Optional: install the browser extension on your computer
3. Link devices with a one-time code and see your activity reports
